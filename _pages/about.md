Hey there!

I'm Jash and I am currently pursuing my Masters in [Computer Science](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} at [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from [University of Mumbai](https://www.djsce.ac.in/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, India. 

My research interests lie in natural language processing (NLP) and Federated Learning, particularly at its intersection with computational social science and healthcare. Currently, I am working with [Professor Ling Liu](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~lingliu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} on data poisoning attacks in federated learning systems.

Previously, I collaborated with [Dr. Zeerak Talat](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=3M3WdvkAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (Digital Democracies Institute at SFU) on improving hate speech detection systems. Before that, I was a Research Intern under [Prof. Mayank Singh](https://mayank4490.github.io/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} at [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Gandhinagar](https://iitgn.ac.in/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, exploring interpretability for neural NLP. At Unicode Research, I collaborated with [Swapneel Mehta](https://swapneelm.github.io/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (NYU CSMaP group) and [Akash Srivastava](https://mitibmwatsonailab.mit.edu/people/akash-srivastava/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} (MIT-IBM AI lab). I have been fortunate to work with such brilliant mentors and grateful to them for their advice and support. 

I'm passionate about knowledge transfer and serve as a mentor at [DJ Unicode](https://www.djunicode.in/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, a student organization that encourages budding CS sophomores and juniors to contribute to open source projects and find their calling in tech. I was a Teaching Assistant for the [Unicode Machine Learning Summer Course 2021](https://djunicode.github.io/umlsc-2021/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} supported by Google Research India.

In my leisure time, I love high-altitude trekking and visiting beautiful, unexplored places. I also enjoy playing table tennis, chess, and reading novels.
